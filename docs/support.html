ByeAI Help

ByeAI blocks AI features on websites before they load. You stay in control.

How do I allow AI on one site?

Click the ByeAI icon in Chrome, then click Allow AI on this site.

Why is a site acting broken?

Allow AI on that site, or turn ByeAI off from the popup toggle.

Does ByeAI read my pages?

No. ByeAI blocks known AI loaders. It does not scan your text or images.
