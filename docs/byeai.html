ByeAI

Bye, AI until you allow it.

ByeAI is a Chrome extension that blocks AI features on websites before they load. Like an ad blocker, but for AI. You choose which sites can use AI.

What it does

  • Blocks AI chat widgets, floating help bots, and customer-support AI
  • Blocks embedded copilots and "Ask AI" / "Summarize with AI" panels added by sites
  • Blocks Google AI Overviews and other AI search summaries
  • Blocks background AI service API calls so sites can't quietly stream answers
  • Shows how many items were blocked on the current page
  • Lets you allow AI on any site with one click

What it does not do

  • Does not scan whether text or images are AI generated
  • Does not send your browsing history to LAFU
