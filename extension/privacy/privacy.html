ByeAI Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 29, 2026

Extension: ByeAI: block AI across the web

Summary
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not scan or analyze your page text, images, audio, or video.
  • We do not send your browsing history to our servers.
  • Your settings stay on your device in Chrome storage.

This Privacy Policy describes how ByeAI ("we", "us", "the extension") handles information when you use the ByeAI Chrome extension. ByeAI helps you block AI chat widgets, copilots, and AI service calls from loading on websites you visit.

1. Who we are

ByeAI is published by Maximum Effort AY, a company in Finland. LAFU is the product studio behind ByeAI (lafu.fi). The developer contact details on the Chrome Web Store listing apply to privacy inquiries.

2. What ByeAI does

ByeAI blocks known AI related network requests and may hide known AI widget elements on a page. It uses:

  • Chrome declarativeNetRequest rules to block AI widget scripts, embeds, and AI API calls
  • Lightweight page rules to hide known AI widget shells if they still appear

ByeAI does not detect whether content was written or generated by AI. It does not upload page content for analysis.

3. Information we collect

We do not collect personal information from you.

ByeAI does not require an account and does not operate backend servers that receive your browsing data.

4. Information stored on your device

The extension stores the following locally in Chrome storage on your device only:

  • Whether ByeAI blocking is turned on or off
  • A list of websites where you chose to allow AI
  • A count of blocked items on the current page session
  • A running total count of blocked items since install
  • Minor UI preferences, such as whether a help tip was dismissed

This data stays in your browser profile. We cannot access it remotely unless you choose to share information with us directly, for example by sending a support email.

5. Information we do not collect

  • Name, email address, or other contact details (unless you email us)
  • Authentication credentials
  • Full browsing history sent to our servers
  • Page text, images, audio, video, or form content for analysis
  • Payment or financial information
  • Precise location data
  • Health information

6. Why Chrome permissions are used

ByeAI requests permissions only to provide blocking features:

  • Website access (http and https): apply blocking and cosmetic rules on pages you visit, and show stats for the active tab.
  • declarativeNetRequest: block known AI widget and AI service URLs before they load.
  • declarativeNetRequestFeedback: show accurate blocked counts in the popup.
  • storage: save your settings, allowed sites, and counters locally.
  • tabs: read the active tab hostname for the popup and Allow AI on this site.

Chrome may show a notice that the extension can read and change data on websites you visit. That notice appears because ByeAI runs on web pages to block AI loaders. ByeAI does not exfiltrate your browsing history to us.

7. Third parties

ByeAI does not include third party analytics, advertising SDKs, social trackers, or remote AI detection services.

If we add optional remote filter list updates in a future version, those updates would fetch rule definitions only. They would not include your personal data. This policy will be updated before any such feature ships.

Your use of Chrome and the Chrome Web Store is also subject to Google's policies.

8. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data. We do not share your locally stored extension settings with third parties for advertising or profiling.

9. Data retention and deletion

Data stored by ByeAI remains on your device until you remove it by uninstalling the extension, clearing extension storage, resetting counters in settings, or removing allowed sites from the options page.

10. Your choices

  • Turn ByeAI on or off from the popup
  • Allow AI on a specific site while keeping protection elsewhere
  • Remove allowed sites from the options page
  • Reset the total blocked counter in settings
  • Uninstall the extension to remove all locally stored ByeAI data

11. Children's privacy

ByeAI is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. International users

ByeAI stores settings locally on your device. No personal data is transferred to us by default. If you contact us, we will process only the information you provide in your message.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the extension changes. The effective date at the top will be revised. Material changes will also be reflected in the Chrome Web Store listing where required.

14. Contact

For privacy questions, contact hello@lafu.fi or visit lafu.fi/privacy.
