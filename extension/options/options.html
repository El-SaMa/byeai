Welcome

ByeAI blocks AI on websites before it loads: chat widgets, copilots, Google AI Overviews, AI buttons like Grok and Meta AI, and background AI calls. It can also blur posts and videos that sites label as AI. Like an ad blocker, but for AI.

  • Blocking is on by default.
  • Click the ByeAI icon to see what was blocked on the current page.
  • Use Allow AI on this site when you actually want a site's AI features.
  • Site acting weird? Allow AI on that site or turn ByeAI off from the popup.

Everything happens on your device. ByeAI reads the AI labels sites already show and never sends your pages anywhere.

        Privacy vs UI

        What ByeAI actually stops, in plain language.

        • Real blocking (privacy) Stops AI scripts and API calls before they run. Used for chat widgets, copilots, and AI service connections on most sites.
        • Google Search Switches results to Web mode and blocks known AI fetch URLs. This reduces AI on the page, but Google still receives your search query like normal search.
        • UI hiding (backup) Hides AI boxes that still appear on screen. This is mainly so you do not see them. Network blocking does the heavy work.

        ByeAI only looks for AI labels and tags on the page, on your device. Your browsing is never sent to us. It does not make browsing anonymous. For stronger search privacy, use a search engine you trust or turn ByeAI off where you need full site features.

        Permissions

        ByeAI needs access to websites you visit so it can block AI loaders before they run. Your settings follow you to your other computers through Chrome sync. Your browsing never goes to us.

        • declarativeNetRequest: block known AI widget and API URLs
        • storage: save your settings and allowed sites
        • alarms: download the ByeAI Community list once a day
        • webNavigation: switch Google searches to Web results
